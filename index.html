Risk & consistency
Risk profile — on weekly settled NAV

All risk statistics are computed on weekly returns (√52 annualization, calendar 365-day basis) — the honest frequency given ~weekly settlement. Volatility, Sharpe and Sortino computed on the interpolated daily series would be smoothing-biased (understate risk, overstate ratios), so they are deliberately not shown. Drawdowns are measured on the real settlement path. Sharpe/Sortino are excess over the risk-free rate set above.

Methodology & data
How these numbers are computed
NAV per share
Read live from the Lagoon vault (pricePerShare). The valuation manager settles total assets roughly weekly; NAV per share is derived at each settlement. Already net of fees.
Annualized yield
(P_end / P_start)365/d − 1, the trailing-window annualized growth of NAV per share (the Superstate / Ondo convention). Net of fees.
CAGR (since inception)
(P_now / P_inception)1/years − 1, geometric — the constant annual rate reproducing the realized end value.
Interpolation
The daily line is linear interpolation of settlement points at UTC midnights, drawn for readability. No extrapolation past the last settlement. Statistics never use the interpolated daily series.
